## In short

A short weekly chat with {child} about how the week went, the next step down, and the family's rules.

## How to use it

1. Pick a calm time each week, for example after the week resets.
2. Go through the list on the Family screen together.
3. Talk about the next step, then tick "Agreed at the family review" if you both agree.

## Why it helps

Rules that come with reasons, and with a say for the child, are linked to children taking them on and pushing back less, especially from about age 10. A few agreed rules, revisited often, are easier to keep than a long list.

## What {child} sees

Your week is 6 h 30 from Sunday. You agreed this at the review.

## Watch out for

Too many rules. Three to five, each with its reason, is plenty. Keep it short, and keep it a conversation rather than a verdict.

## The research

Valkenburg and colleagues, 2013, Human Communication Research. Rules given with reasons and a say for the child were linked to children taking them on, pushing back less and hiding less. These studies show a link, not proof of cause.
