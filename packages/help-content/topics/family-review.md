## In short

A short weekly chat with {child} about how the week went, the savings goal, the next step down, and the family's rules.

## How to use it

1. Pick a calm time each week before the week resets, for example Sunday afternoon.
2. Go through the list on the Family screen together.
3. If there's a next step, talk it through first. Tick "Agreed at the family review" only if you and {child} agree.

## Why it helps

Rules that come with reasons, and with a say for the child, are linked to children taking them on and pushing back less. It matters most from about age 10.

## What {child} sees

From Sunday your week is 6 h 30. You agreed this at the family review.

## Watch out for

Too many rules. Three to five, each with its reason, is plenty. Keep it short, and keep it a conversation rather than a verdict.

A step agreed after the week has reset waits until the next week.

## The research

Valkenburg and colleagues, 2013, Human Communication Research. Rules given with reasons and a say for the child were linked to children taking them on, pushing back less and hiding less. This shows a link, not proof of cause.
