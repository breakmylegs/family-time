## In short

Stops managed apps on {their} devices, within about a minute.

## How to use it

1. Tap Pause everything.
2. Choose how long, or until you resume.
3. Tap Resume now when you're done.

## Why it helps

Useful for meals, family time and the run-up to bed. Screen-free routines like these are linked to better sleep.

## What {child} sees

Screens are paused for now. Back at 19:00.

## Watch out for

Using it as a punishment. Studies link using screens as a reward or punishment to children using screens more, not less.

## The research

Carter and colleagues, 2016, JAMA Pediatrics: using devices at bedtime was linked to poorer sleep. The UK Chief Medical Officers advise screen-free mealtimes. Nagata and colleagues, 2024, Pediatric Research: screens used as a reward or a punishment were linked to more use.
