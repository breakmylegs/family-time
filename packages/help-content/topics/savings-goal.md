## In short

Something {child} is saving for, with its price. The bar shows how close {child} is, from the pocket money earned since the goal was set.

## How to use it

1. Let {child} choose what to save for, for example bike lights.
2. On the Family screen, tap Set a goal, then name it and set what it costs.
3. Look at it together at the family review, and change it if {child} wants something else.

## How it works

The bar fills with the pocket money {child} earns from the day the goal is set, up to its price: money from jobs, flex points turned into pocket money, and the unused-time bonus, plus anything you add by matching. Marking money as paid doesn't move the bar back, so it only ever goes forward. Setting a new goal starts a new bar; changing the price of the same goal keeps it.

If you want to add a share yourselves, choose it under You add when you set or change the goal. When each week ends, Family time adds that share of the pocket money {child} earned that week (in the goal's first week, only since it was set), as its own line called Matched by Mum and Dad, never more than the goal still needs. It is your money, so pay it out with the rest.

## Watch out for

Paying out all the money as it is earned. The bar still fills, but {child} may not have the money when the goal is reached. Talk about keeping some aside until then.
