## In short

An assistant such as Claude can connect to Family time to tell you what each child can use now, and to suggest changes. Its suggestions are cards on Approvals: it can never change a rule by itself.

## How to use it

1. The owner turns on Let apps connect in Settings, under AI and connected apps.
2. In the assistant, add a connector with the address shown there.
3. The assistant opens Family time. Check what it asks for, then tap Yes, connect it.

## How it works

The owner connects each app first. After that, other parents can connect it too, each with their own sign-in. Carers can't connect apps. Only the owner can give an app more, such as suggesting as well as seeing.

An app can ask to see, or to see and suggest. Seeing covers what is allowed now, the week, today's changes and what is waiting on Approvals. Before a suggestion is sent, the assistant asks you to confirm it.

A sign-in ends after 30 days without use. To stop an app at once, tap Remove next to it in Settings.

## Watch out for

What the app shows you goes to the assistant's company, including your children's names, their week and their pocket money. Connect only an assistant you already trust with family details.

If the assistant can't connect, it may not support the newest connector version yet.
