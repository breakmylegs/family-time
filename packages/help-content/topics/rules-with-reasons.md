## In short

{child} can ask about any rule from the kiosk, at any time. You answer with the reason, or put it on the family review to talk about together.

## How to use it

1. A question shows on Approvals.
2. Write your answer, giving the reason for the rule, and tap Send the answer.
3. Or tap Add to the family review. It goes on the agenda, and you answer it on Family once you have talked.

## How it works

{child} can have three questions waiting at once. Answers stay on the kiosk for two weeks. Only parents see the questions, and they are never sent to AI.

## Why it helps

Rules made with reasons, recognising the child's view, are linked to children taking them on, pushing back less and hiding less. It matters most from about age 10.

## What {child} sees

{Their} question, whether it is waiting or on the family review, and your answer.

## Watch out for

An answer doesn't have to be yes. A clear reason is the part that helps.

## The research

Valkenburg and others, 2013, Human Communication Research. Rules set with reasons and a say for the child were linked to children taking them on, pushing back less and hiding less.
