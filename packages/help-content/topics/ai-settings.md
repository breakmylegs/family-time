## In short

AI is optional and starts off. When it is on, it only ever suggests: nothing changes until a parent approves or saves it. Everything in Family time works without it.

## How to use it

1. As the owner, open Settings and turn on Use AI. Only the owner sees these settings.
2. Set up a provider: paste its key, or for Ollama, give the address of your own server.
3. Turn on each feature you want, pick its provider and model, and set a monthly limit.

## How it works

Your keys are locked away on your server and never come back to the app: only the last four characters show. If the server has no master key, AI stays off, and so do connected apps.

Each feature says what it sends. Job ideas send the age bands and titles of your jobs, the month and the weather. The week note sends totals only. Plain-English control sends what you type, your app and group names and the time, with your children's names replaced by Child A and Child B; for a cloud provider, only once you turn that on. Photos, the sites a device visits and {child}'s own answers never go to a cloud provider.

Each feature stops for the rest of the month when it reaches its limit, and goes back to working without AI. Every call is logged with an estimate of its cost, but what was sent and what came back are not kept.

## Watch out for

Google's free plan uses what you send to improve its products, so Family time only uses a Google key you say is on a paid plan.
