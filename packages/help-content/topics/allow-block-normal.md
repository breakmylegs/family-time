## In short

Each app follows {child}'s plan and your family rules. Allow and Block let you step in for a while.

## How to use it

1. Allow lets an app work for the time you choose.
2. Block stops it for the time you choose.
3. Normal goes back to the plan and your family rules.

## How it works

Blocking works on your home Wi-Fi. Changes take up to a minute, and a video that's already playing may finish first. Screen Time on an iPad still applies as a backup.

Allow and Block always end on their own. If you block an app during time {child} planned, the planned time pauses and the minutes not used go back to {them}.

## What {child} sees

Roblox is on until 19:02. Mum said yes.

## Watch out for

Allowing apps on top of the allowance most days. If it keeps happening, talk about the allowance at the family review instead.
