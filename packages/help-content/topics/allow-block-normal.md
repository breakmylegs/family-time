## In short

Each app follows {child}'s plan and your family rules. Allow and Block let you step in for a while.

## How to use it

1. Allow lets an app work for the time you choose.
2. Block stops it for the time you choose.
3. Normal goes back to {their} plan and your family rules.

## How it works

Blocking works on your home Wi-Fi. Changes take up to a minute, and a video that's already playing may finish first. Screen Time on {their} iPad still applies as a backup.

## What {child} sees

Roblox is on until 19:02. Mum said yes.

## Watch out for

Allowing apps on top of {their} allowance most days. If it keeps happening, talk about the allowance at the family review instead.
