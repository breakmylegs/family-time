## In short

A suggestion is a card on Approvals. Nothing changes until a parent taps Approve, and then it is that parent's own change, just as if they had tapped Allow, Block or Pause on Control, or Approve on Approvals.

## How to use it

1. If the owner has turned it on, type what you want on Control in your own words, for example "YouTube for an hour", then tap Make a card to approve.
2. Open Approvals. Read the card, then tap Approve or Dismiss.
3. Changed your mind after approving? Set it back on Control: tap Normal, or Resume for a Pause.

## How it works

Suggestions come from AI or from a connected app, such as an assistant on your phone. Each card says where it came from and shows what was asked. Only the family's own children, apps and the usual times can be on a card. A card closes after 30 minutes if nobody answers it, because an Allow suggested at teatime means little by bedtime.

Carers can approve a Pause or a yes to {child}'s request, but only a parent can approve an Allow or a Block.

Job ideas, on Jobs, never go to Approvals. Tap one to open it, check it, and save it yourself. Nothing is added by itself. The note on the week, on Family, is written from the totals only.

## What {child} sees

Nothing from AI. There is no AI on the kiosk, and none that talks to {child}.
