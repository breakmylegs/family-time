## In short

The weekly allowance comes down a little at a time, in steps you agree with {child}.

## How to use it

1. Choose how big each step is and how often it happens.
2. Set a floor the allowance won't go below.
3. Talk about each step at the family review before it starts.

## Why it helps

Small, agreed steps are easier to stick to than a sudden cut, and they give {them} a say.

## What {child} sees

From 9 November your week is 6 h 30. You agreed this on Sunday.

## Watch out for

Skipping the conversation. A cut that arrives without warning feels like a punishment.

## The research

Epstein and colleagues, 2008. The budget was cut by 10% of the starting amount each month until it reached half. Research on rule-setting also finds children push back less when rules come with reasons and a say in them (Valkenburg and colleagues, 2013).
