## In short

Everyone signs in with their own passkey, and their role decides what they can change.

## How it works

The owner can do everything, including devices and system settings. Parents can allow, block and pause, approve jobs and requests, and change the family's rules. Carers, for example grandparents, can see what's on, pause everything and approve requests within the plan, but can't change rules.

Children never sign in. They use the kiosk.

## Watch out for

Sharing one sign-in between two people. Invite each person, so Today shows who did what.
