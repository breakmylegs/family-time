## In short

Chores earn pocket money or flex points by default. Screen time as a reward is capped.

## How to use it

1. A new job starts on pocket money. Keep it, or choose flex points under What it earns.
2. Choose screen time only now and then. Screen time from jobs, and from flex points turned into screen time, is capped at 15% of the weekly allowance each week.
3. Once the cap is reached, approving a screen-time job pays only what is left of it. The approval card says so before you tap.

## Why it helps

When screen time is the prize, it tends to become more wanted. Keeping most rewards in money or flex points keeps the focus on the job.

## Watch out for

Making screen time the reward for every job. The cap is there so most of {child}'s screen time stays the agreed allowance, not a prize.

## The research

Tang and colleagues 2019, and Nagata and colleagues 2024, linked using screens as a reward or a punishment to more screen use. Those two studies observed families, so they show a link, not proof of cause. In experiments by Birch and colleagues, a food repeatedly given as a reward became more liked.
