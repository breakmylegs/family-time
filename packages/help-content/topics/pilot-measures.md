## In short

A few numbers for each week of the pilot, so you can see what is changing over the 16 weeks rather than guess. These are for you and the family review. {child} sees only {their} goal, {their} savings and the time {they} saved this week.

## How to use it

1. Open Family before the family review and look at this week next to the last few.
2. Talk through what changed, as a conversation rather than a verdict.
3. Tap Show all to see every week, or Download as a spreadsheet to keep them.

## How it works

Every number comes from what Family time already keeps, counted by the week you set for the allowance:

- Screen time is minutes on {child}'s devices using each app Family time manages, from the home Wi-Fi's records. A minute with two apps counts for each, so it is close but not exact.
- Wind-down and bedtime show minutes of use during those parts of {child}'s timetable as it is now.
- Unused time that became pocket money is the time left at the end of the week, once the unused-time bonus is on.
- Jobs and ideas shows the jobs approved, and how many different jobs plus new Bored? ideas there were.
- How sessions ended counts your taps of calm, grumble or meltdown after extra time ends, or a block or pause starts.

Numbers only appear for weeks Family time was running, and some stay at zero until the pilot phase that switches them on.

## Watch out for

Using the numbers as a score. A week with more screen time is a reason to ask what happened, not a reason to take something away.
