## In short

What {child} has earned in money and you haven't handed over yet. Family time keeps count; the money itself stays with you.

## How to use it

1. Jobs set to pocket money add to it when you approve them. So do flex points you convert to pocket money, and the unused-time bonus if it's switched on.
2. Pay {child} the way you normally would, in cash or into {their} account.
3. On the Family screen, tap Mark as paid and choose how much you handed over.

## How it works

Pocket money owed is a running total. Marking money as paid takes it off the total and adds a line to the history, so the history shows both what was earned and what was paid.

Nothing is ever taken off as a punishment. If a job's pay or a conversion was added by mistake, or you marked too much as paid, use Fix a mistake beside that line in the history. That undoes exactly that amount, and both lines stay in the history. It can't take back more than is still owed, so put it right before you pay. The unused-time bonus can't be changed.

## Watch out for

Forgetting to mark money as paid. The total only goes down when you tap Mark as paid, so it can look as if you owe more than you do.
