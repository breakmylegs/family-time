## In short

Family time can tell you when something needs you, even when the app is closed.

## How to use it

1. On iPhone, add Family time to your Home Screen first: tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
2. Open it from the Home Screen and go to Settings.
3. Tap Turn on alerts, then Allow.

## How it works

For now you're told when a change can't be confirmed on {child}'s devices, and when a device goes quiet. Tapping an alert opens the screen it's about. Alerts travel through Apple's or Google's push service and say only what's needed.

## Watch out for

Alerts turned off in your phone's own settings. Family time can't see that, so check there if alerts stop arriving.
