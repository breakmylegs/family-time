## In short

A set amount of screen time for the week, agreed with {child}, who plans how to use it.

## How to use it

1. Agree the amount together at the family review.
2. {child} plans the time on the kiosk, in 30-minute blocks.
3. It resets every Sunday at 18:00.

## Why it helps

Studies link using screen time as a reward or a punishment to children using screens more, not less. A weekly allowance keeps screen time a plan rather than a prize, and gives {them} practice at budgeting.

## What {child} sees

You've got 5 h 30 left to plan this week.

## Watch out for

Topping it up as a reward for good behaviour. That turns screen time back into a prize.

## The research

Tang and colleagues, 2019, BMC Obesity, and Nagata and colleagues, 2024, Pediatric Research. In both, parents using screens to reward or punish was linked to more screen use. These show a link, not proof of cause.
