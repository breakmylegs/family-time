## In short

{child} can ask on the kiosk for an app at a set time, for example Roblox from 19:00 to 19:30. You say yes or not this time.

## How to use it

1. The request shows up in Approvals.
2. Tap Yes to plan it. The time comes off {child}'s weekly allowance, and the app opens for that slot.
3. Or tap Not this time. {child} sees your answer on the kiosk.

## How it works

If {child} hasn't enough weekly time left for it, only a parent can say yes, as extra time. Extra time doesn't come off the week.

If the time asked for doesn't fit your family's rules, for example it's outside free time, in wind-down or over the app's daily limit, Approvals says why and you can only say not this time. To make an exception, a parent can use Allow on the Control screen when the time comes. An Allow can't override a pause, and stops at bedtime unless you choose Until tomorrow.

Carers can say yes from the weekly time, but only a parent can give extra time.

## Watch out for

Saying yes as extra time most days. If it keeps happening, talk about the allowance at the family review instead.
