## In short

Points {child} earns from jobs, to turn into pocket money or screen time later. You and {child} choose together what the points become.

## How to use it

1. On the Jobs screen, choose Flex points under What it earns when you add or change a job.
2. When {child} wants to use them, tap Convert on the Family screen and choose pocket money or screen time.
3. If something went wrong that wasn't {child}'s fault, for example the Wi-Fi went down, tap Make up for a problem under History on the Family screen.

## How it works

Each point turns into a set amount: by default 1p of pocket money or 1 minute of screen time. Right now shows your family's rates.

Screen time from points counts towards the same weekly cap as screen time from jobs: 15% of the weekly allowance. If converting would go over the cap, Family time asks you to convert fewer points or choose pocket money. Screen time from points is for this week only, like the rest of the week's time.

If you stop a planned session, its time goes back to {child}'s week on its own. Make up for a problem is for anything else that went wrong.

## Watch out for

Taking points away. Family time has no way to take points off as a punishment. If points were added by mistake, use Fix a mistake beside them in the history, which undoes exactly that entry. Points already converted can't be taken back.
