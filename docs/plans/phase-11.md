# Phase 11 plan: enhancements from the research

The owner asked for every worthwhile enhancement, decided on their behalf. The source is the research's own lists (docs/research/03-motivation.md: V1 must-have, V1 nice-to-have, V2, ethical guardrails, metrics), checked against what phases 0 to 10 built.

## What gets built

| # | Item | Research source | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Platform setup checklist | V1 must-have: "YouTube autoplay off and Roblox daily limit setup checklist" (Hiniker 2016, platform docs); UK CMO guidance on phones outside the bedroom | Per child on Set-up, ticked by parents; items depend on the child's age band |
| 2 | The child asks about a rule | Guardrail: "The child can request a rule change at any time through the kiosk" | Kiosk "Ask about a rule"; a card on Approvals; Talk about it at the review puts it on the agenda |
| 3 | Unmanaged screen time, the parents' estimate | Metrics: "Unmanaged screen use (parent estimate), weekly, watch for displacement" | A weekly estimate per child on Family; a column in the pilot measures |
| 4 | Offline activity journal | V1 nice-to-have: "Offline activity journal, praise not points" (Deci 1999) | Kiosk "Something I did"; parents see it on Family and can send praise; counts towards offline variety |
| 5 | Things I can do | V1 nice-to-have: "Skill badges for real mastery" (SDT competence) | The child adds a skill they are learning; a parent confirms it once mastered; shown on the kiosk. No points |
| 6 | Monthly mood check | Metrics: wellbeing check-in, monthly; "for under-8s, use a parent-reported mood check" | A parent's monthly five-point check with a note, in the pilot measures. Not a validated scale, and labelled so |
| 7 | Age bands | Age bands table; V2 "Age-band presets" | 5 to 7, 8 to 10, 11 to 13, 14 and over, per child; drives the checklist and its guidance |
| 8 | Children's Code self-assessment and DPIA template | V2 documentation | docs/privacy/ |

## Not built, with reasons

- Co-play windows: an Allow can already be set not to count against the week.
- The child wellbeing scale: its licence is still the owner's to confirm.
- Teen self-management mode, differential pricing: V2, and pricing only if pilot data justify it.
- Evaluated and not recommended by the research: compounding interest, leaderboards, loss streaks, countdowns, child-facing AI.

## Rules

No points for any of it (praise, never prizes). Nothing visible to siblings about each other. The kiosk keeps "What Mum and Dad can see" true: it lists the new things parents see. Every new table is in the export; migrations stay additive.
